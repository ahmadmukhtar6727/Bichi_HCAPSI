Bichi Central Ward Community HIV Screening & PMTCT Awareness Campaign

Implementation Period: August 2026
Location: Bichi Central Ward
Target Community: Adults & Expectant Mothers
Lead Body: BHCAPSI

Background & Objectives

To address gaps in voluntary counseling and testing (HTS) and promote early antenatal booking for PMTCT, BHCAPSI conducted a one-day mobile health outreach in collaboration with local primary health centers.

Activities Executed

  • Community health talks on HIV transmission myths, U=U, and stigma reduction in English and Hausa.
  • Voluntary, confidential HIV counseling and testing (HTS) provided by trained health facility staff.
  • Direct referral linkages established for individuals requiring ongoing clinical care or PMTCT services.

Verified Results & Impact

✔️ 140+ Community Members Reached: Provided with voluntary testing and educational guidance.
✔️ 100% Linkage Rate: All identified individuals requiring care were successfully referred to supported primary health centers in Bichi.

Hypertension & Diabetes Awareness Drive

Implementation Period: Q3 – Q4 2026
Location: Multiple Wards across Bichi LGA
Target Community: Adults (Ages 30+)
Focus: Cardiovascular & NCD Health

Objectives & Activities

Promoting routine blood pressure and blood sugar checks across community gathering points. Educating residents on healthy dietary choices, reducing salt intake, and maintaining regular follow-up with qualified health providers.
