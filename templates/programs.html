1. Sickle Cell Anaemia Awareness & Genotype Counseling

Our initiative works across Bichi LGA to raise public awareness on Sickle Cell Disease (SCD), encourage premarital genotype testing (AA, AS, SS), and support affected individuals and families through community education, crisis prevention guidance, and hospital linkages.

  • Promoting premarital genotype screening campaigns & counseling for intended couples.
  • Community dialogues to break myths and eliminate social stigma around genetic disorders.
  • Guidance on infection prevention, proper hydration, and crisis management for warriors.
  • Connecting families with healthcare facilities for routine management and care.

2. HIV Prevention, Awareness & Referral Support

We provide comprehensive HIV prevention education, promote voluntary counseling and testing (HTS) awareness, and emphasize confidentiality and informed consent. Our initiatives aim to eliminate social stigma and help individuals identify trusted health centers for testing, counseling, and long-term care.

  • Promoting free HIV Testing and Counseling (HTS) across supported centers.
  • Awareness on Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission (PMTCT) for pregnant mothers.
  • Education on Undetectable = Untransmittable (U=U) and Anti-Retroviral Therapy (ART) adherence.
  • Confidential community support and anti-stigma campaigns.

3. Communicable Disease Prevention

Providing general health education on communicable diseases affecting Bichi LGA and surrounding communities. We focus on preventive measures, early symptom identification, and timely medical evaluation to curb community transmission.

  • Tuberculosis (TB): Screening education, cough hygiene, and referral for free diagnosis.
  • Malaria: Vector control education, bed net utilization, and prompt testing.
  • Hepatitis & STIs: Prevention strategies, safe hygiene practices, and treatment pathways.
🫀

4. Non-Communicable Disease (NCD) Awareness

Promoting community education on chronic conditions such as hypertension, diabetes, and cardiovascular diseases. We emphasize early detection, routine blood pressure checks, balanced nutrition, and ongoing medical management.

  • Understanding risk factors, lifestyle choices, and dietary adjustments.
  • Encouraging routine blood pressure and blood sugar screenings at local health facilities.
  • Guidance on medication compliance and follow-up care for chronic conditions.
📢

5. Community Outreach & Health Education

Supporting grass-roots health awareness activities, community dialogues, and stakeholder engagement across Bichi wards. We work alongside local leaders, traditional institutions, and community groups to share accurate health facts in English and Hausa.

  • Educational sessions at primary health centers and community gatherings.
  • Collaborative campaigns with local health advocates and volunteers.
  • Verified post-event activity reporting to measure outcome and community reach.
🏥

6. Linkage to Appropriate Health Services

Providing reliable guidance on where to seek qualified medical care. We assist community members in navigating local primary healthcare centers, general hospitals, and specialized clinical care pathways across Bichi Local Government Area.
