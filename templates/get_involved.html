Get Involved & Partner With Us

Building healthier communities in Bichi Local Government Area through collective action, institutional collaboration, and local advocacy.

Partner With Us

Bichi Healthier Community, Awareness, Prevention & Support Initiative welcomes opportunities to connect with organizations and individuals who share a commitment to healthier, better-informed communities in Kano State.

Potential Collaboration Areas:

📍 Community Outreach Joint health education campaigns and grass-roots disease prevention awareness.
🏥 Health Facility Linkage Strengthening referral pathways for HIV testing, PMTCT, and non-communicable disease care.
🎓 Stakeholder Training Capacity-building workshops for local health advocates, youth groups, and community leaders.
🙋‍♂️

Volunteer With Us

Community health improvement requires local participation. We invite health professionals, student advocates, civil society leaders, and community members in Bichi LGA to volunteer.

Volunteer Eligibility & Code of Conduct:

  • Commitment to non-discrimination, confidentiality, and respect for all community members.
  • Willingness to support community health education and awareness sessions across local wards.
  • Compliance with national data protection guidelines and BHCAPSI ethics.
