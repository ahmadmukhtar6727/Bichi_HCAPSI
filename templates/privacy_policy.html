Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Adhering to the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 & BHCAPSI Safeguarding Principles

⚠️ Notice Regarding Personal Health Data

The BHCAPSI web portal is an educational, awareness, and community referral system. It is not a clinical patient-record or diagnostic platform. Please do not submit medical test results, detailed medical histories, or clinical records through our general public web forms.

Effective Date: October 2026 | Jurisdiction: Federal Republic of Nigeria (NDPA 2023)

1. Principles of Data Protection

Bichi Healthier Community, Awareness, Prevention & Support Initiative (BHCAPSI) is committed to handling all personal information in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023. Our data processing adheres strictly to the principles of lawful processing, purpose specification, data minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, and confidential integrity.

2. Data We Collect

We process minimal personal information submitted voluntarily by visitors through our web platform:

  • Contact & Referral Inquiries: Name, email address, message subject, and message content provided during general inquiries.
  • Anonymous Inquiries: If you select our "Submit Anonymously" mode, no name or email address is collected or associated with your message text.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Standard security log data (such as server request logs) maintained for web performance and security protection without individual profiling.

3. Confidentiality & Anonymous Safeguards

Respecting human dignity and eliminating stigma—particularly regarding HIV prevention, testing, and care—are core values of BHCAPSI. All messages submitted to our admin portal are accessible only to designated, authorized public health personnel bound by confidentiality duties.

4. Purpose of Data Usage

Personal data provided through this site is used solely to:

  • Respond to community inquiries regarding health education and local facility locations.
  • Facilitate appropriate health facility referrals across Bichi Local Government Area.
  • Process partnership and volunteer inquiries.

5. Data Subject Rights Under NDPA 2023

As a community member or site user in Nigeria, you hold specific rights under the NDPA 2023 regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: Request confirmation or copies of personal data held about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Request deletion of your contact messages from our administrative portal.
  • Right to Object: Object to processing carried out based on legitimate interests or public interest.

6. Contacting the Data Controller

To exercise any of your data protection rights or submit questions regarding our privacy practices, please contact our administrative team via our confidential channel:

BHCAPSI Data Compliance Desk
