Who We Are

Bichi Healthier Community, Awareness, Prevention and Support Initiative (BHCAPSI) is a community health NGO based in Bichi, Kano State, focused on health education and connecting people to health services.

💬 "Building Healthier Communities Together" — promoting health awareness, preventing disease, and reducing stigma.

Our mandate covers comprehensive health intervention including Neglected Tropical Disease (NTD) awareness and mass drug administration support, genetic health advocacy (Sickle Cell Anaemia screening & counseling), communicable disease control (Malaria, Tuberculosis, Hepatitis), non-communicable chronic disease prevention (Hypertension, Diabetes, CVD), supporting immunization partners to reach zero-dose children, and confidential HIV testing, care linkage, and anti-stigma advocacy.

🌿 Neglected Tropical Diseases (NTDs)

Supporting community awareness, hygiene promotion, and Mass Drug Administration (MDA) linkages to eliminate River Blindness, Lymphatic Filariasis, Schistosomiasis, and Soil-Transmitted Helminths.

🩸 Sickle Cell & Genetic Health

Promoting premarital genotype testing, community awareness on sickle cell trait inheritance, crisis care guidance, and reducing social stigma.

🛡️ Communicable Disease Prevention

Educating communities on vector control for malaria, airborne infection hygiene for tuberculosis, and vaccination and safe practices for viral hepatitis.

🫀 NCD & Lifestyle Wellness

Promoting routine blood pressure and blood sugar screenings, balanced dietary habits, and ongoing medical management for hypertension and diabetes.

🌟

Our Core Values

  • Effective Community Action: Increasing access to reliable health facts, encouraging practical disease prevention practices, and engaging grass-roots stakeholders.
  • Inclusion & Anti-Stigma: Promoting non-discriminatory health access, reducing social stigma around chronic conditions, NTDs, and genetic health, and respecting individual dignity.
  • Confidentiality & Safeguarding: Upholding national data privacy standards (NDPA 2023) and protecting community inquiries with strict administrative confidentiality.
  • Community Linkage & Empowerment: Connecting residents directly to nearby primary health centers and supporting individuals and families towards long-term wellness.
