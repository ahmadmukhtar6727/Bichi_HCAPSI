Who We Are

Bichi Healthier Community, Awareness, Prevention and Support Initiative (BHCAPSI) is a community-based, non-profit organization established to advance public health, disease prevention, and total wellbeing for individuals, families, and communities across Bichi Local Government Area of Kano State.

Our mandate covers comprehensive health intervention including communicable disease control (Malaria, Tuberculosis, Hepatitis), non-communicable chronic disease prevention (Hypertension, Diabetes, CVD), and confidential HIV testing, care linkage, and anti-stigma advocacy.

🛡️ Communicable Disease Prevention

Educating communities on vector control for malaria, airborne infection hygiene for tuberculosis, and vaccination and safe practices for viral hepatitis.

🫀 NCD & Lifestyle Wellness

Promoting routine blood pressure and blood sugar screenings, balanced dietary habits, and ongoing medical management for hypertension and diabetes.

🎗️ HIV Counseling, Care & Anti-Stigma

Ensuring free, voluntary testing (HTS), PMTCT for expectant mothers, U=U awareness, and confidential care linkages across all Bichi health facilities.

🌟

Our Core Values

  • Effective Community Action: Increasing access to reliable health facts, encouraging practical disease prevention practices, and engaging grass-roots stakeholders.
  • Inclusion & Anti-Stigma: Promoting non-discriminatory health access, reducing social stigma around chronic conditions, and respecting individual dignity.
  • Confidentiality & Safeguarding: Upholding national data privacy standards (NDPA 2023) and protecting community inquiries with strict administrative confidentiality.
  • Community Linkage & Empowerment: Connecting residents directly to nearby primary health centers and supporting individuals and families towards long-term wellness.
