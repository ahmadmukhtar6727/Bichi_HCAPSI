What We Offer

Our Vision

A healthy and empowered Bichi community where pandemic (HIV) transmission is reduced, people affected by HIV receive appropriate support, and everyone can access accurate information and essential health services without discrimination.

Our Mission

To mobilize and empower individuals, families and communities in Bichi LGA through HIV awareness, prevention education, testing and referral, community outreach, psychosocial support and collaboration with health and development partners.

Core Programmes

HIV Awareness and Health Education, HIV Prevention, Care and Support, Community Outreach, Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning and HIV Testing and Linkage.
