HIV Awareness, Confidential Testing & Anti-Stigma

HIV is a manageable chronic health condition when identified early and treated with Anti-Retroviral Therapy (ART). Accessing free testing, counseling, and medical care protects individual health and stops transmission across Bichi communities.

1. Confidential HIV Testing (HTS)

Voluntary counseling and rapid screening are provided free of charge at supported primary health centers. Results are kept 100% confidential under national privacy guidelines.

2. Undetectable = Untransmittable (U=U)

People living with HIV who take prescribed ART medications daily can achieve an undetectable viral load. An undetectable viral load means HIV cannot be transmitted to sexual partners.

3. Protecting Babies (PMTCT)

Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission ensures pregnant mothers receive screening and ART during antenatal care, allowing babies to be born completely HIV-free.

🚫 Overcoming Community Stigma

HIV cannot be spread through casual contact, shaking hands, sharing meals, or living in the same house. Discrimination hurts community health by discouraging people from getting tested. Compassion, respect, and early referral save lives.

Communicable Disease Prevention

🦟 Malaria Prevention & Treatment

Malaria remains a leading cause of illness in children and pregnant women. Early testing (RDT) and proper treatment prevent severe complications.

  • Sleep under Long-Lasting Insecticidal Nets (LLINs) every night.
  • Clear stagnant water around compounds to destroy mosquito breeding sites.
  • Seek immediate testing at a health center upon noticing fever, chills, or headache.

🫁 Tuberculosis (TB) Awareness

TB is an airborne bacterial infection affecting the lungs. TB is 100% curable with free medication provided at public facilities.

  • Key Symptom: A cough lasting 2 weeks or more, night sweats, weight loss, or fever.
  • Ensure well-ventilated rooms and practice proper cough hygiene.
  • Complete the full 6-month treatment course to prevent drug-resistant TB.

🧪 Viral Hepatitis (B & C)

Hepatitis affects the liver and can lead to long-term liver damage if left untreated.

  • Get vaccinated against Hepatitis B at your local primary health facility.
  • Avoid sharing unsterilized sharp objects, razor blades, or needles.
  • Screen routinely during antenatal care to protect newborn infants.

Hypertension, Diabetes & Cardiovascular Health

Non-Communicable Diseases (NCDs) develop slowly over time. Because hypertension and diabetes often show no early symptoms, routine screening is vital for prevention and management.

❤️ Hypertension (High BP)

Often called a "silent killer" because high blood pressure can cause strokes or heart attacks without obvious pain. Check your blood pressure regularly at any nearby health center.

🩸 Diabetes Mellitus

High blood sugar can cause kidney disease, vision loss, and nerve damage. Fasting blood sugar tests allow early diagnosis and control.

🫁 Cardiovascular Disease (CVD)

Heart disease risks increase with high salt intake, tobacco use, elevated blood lipids, and physical inactivity. Managed through lifestyle changes and prescribed medication.

🥗

Promoting Healthy Lifestyles & Wellbeing

Small daily habits significantly lower the risk of chronic conditions and strengthen your immune system:

🥦 Balanced Nutrition Eat fresh vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins. Reduce dietary salt, processed sugars, and re-used cooking oils.
🚶 Physical Activity Engage in at least 30 minutes of moderate exercise (brisk walking, domestic work, farming) 5 days a week.
💧 Hydration & Sleep Drink adequate clean water daily and maintain 7–8 hours of rest every night for physical recovery.
🚭 Substance Awareness Avoid tobacco smoke, unprescribed pharmaceutical drugs, and excessive chemical exposure.
