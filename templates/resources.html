HIV/AIDS Education & FAQ

Empowering the Bichi community with accurate health facts, treatment knowledge, and guidance on confidential counseling and care.

🧪 Early Testing

Knowing your HIV status is the first step to living a healthy, full life. Rapid testing is quick, painless, and confidential.

💊 Treatment Works (U=U)

Daily adherence to Anti-Retroviral Therapy (ART) suppresses the virus to undetectable levels, protecting your health and preventing transmission.

👶 Healthy Infants (PMTCT)

Mother-to-child transmission is preventable. Pregnant women receiving early antenatal ART give birth to HIV-negative babies.

Common Myths vs. Medical Facts

Common Myth ❌ Medical Fact ✔️
HIV can be spread by sharing food, handshakes, or hugging. HIV is not transmitted through casual contact, saliva, sweat, tears, or sharing utensils.
An HIV diagnosis is a life sentence. With modern ART medications, individuals with HIV live long, active, and healthy lives.
An HIV-positive mother will always pass the virus to her baby. With effective PMTCT interventions during pregnancy, transmission risk drops to under 1%.
You can tell if someone has HIV just by looking at them. Most people living with HIV look completely healthy. A blood test is the only way to confirm status.
