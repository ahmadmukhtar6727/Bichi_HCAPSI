🎗️ HIV Prevention & Testing Guidance

What is Undetectable = Untransmittable (U=U)?

When a person living with HIV takes prescribed Anti-Retroviral Therapy (ART) regularly and reduces their viral load to an undetectable level, they cannot transmit HIV to their sexual partners.

What is PMTCT?

Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission (PMTCT) includes counseling, testing, and ART provided during pregnancy and breastfeeding to ensure babies are born HIV-free.

🫀 Hypertension & Diabetes Education

Understanding Hypertension (High Blood Pressure)

Hypertension is often called a "silent condition" because it may show no symptoms until checked. Regular blood pressure screenings at local primary health centers help prevent stroke and heart complications.

Healthy Living Habits

  • Reduce daily dietary salt intake.
  • Engage in regular physical activity.
  • Conduct routine blood pressure and glucose checks at supported health centers.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is HIV testing free at supported health centers?

Yes, voluntary HIV testing and counseling (HTS) is provided free of charge across public primary health centers in Bichi Local Government Area.
